▶️ 急性腦癇發作處理動畫

守望相助 急救指南

守 (Stay calm): 保持鎮定，記錄抽搐開始及持續的時間。

望 (Observe): 觀察並保護患者。移開附近危險物品，用軟物墊著患者頭部。

相 (Recovery position): 將患者輕輕翻側，保持呼吸道暢通。切勿強行將任何物品塞入患者口中。

助 (Seek help): 尋求協助。陪伴患者直至清醒，若超過五分鐘請召喚救護車。

📲 安裝及使用指南 ▼
如何安裝至手機 (How to Install)
  • iPhone (iOS): 使用 Safari 瀏覽器開啟此網頁，點擊底部中央的「分享」圖示，然後選擇「加入主畫面」。
  • Android: 使用 Chrome 瀏覽器開啟，點擊右上角「選單」(三點)，然後選擇「加到主畫面」。
如何操作 (How to Operate)
  • 教育模式: 為預設模式。點擊顏色按鍵，手機會播放該步驟的急救指示。
  • 玩具模式: 點擊「錄音模式：關閉」將其開啟。點擊任何顏色按鍵錄製聲音（最長60秒），再次點擊停止。關閉後點擊按鍵即可播放。
💡 什麼是 Be Aware n' Be Around? ▼

"Be Aware of the time. Be Around for the safe recovery."

"Be Aware: Don't restrain. Be Around: Protect and remain."

"Aware of what to do, Around when it matters most."

Be Aware (Mental Vigilance & Safety Knowledge)
  • Recognize & Time: Note when the seizure starts; tracking duration is critical (call emergency services if it exceeds 5 minutes).
  • Know the "Don'ts": Stay mindful to never restrain movement and never place anything in the person's mouth.
  • Assess Environment: Spot immediate physical hazards in the surroundings (sharp corners, water, stairs).
Be Around (Physical Protection & Care)
  • Secure the Surroundings: Clear hard or sharp objects and place something soft under their head.
  • Position safely: Turn the person gently onto their side (recovery position) once jerking stops to keep their airway clear.
  • Provide Post-Seizure Support: Stay by their side through the post-ictal confusion phase until they are fully alert, offering calm reassurance.
